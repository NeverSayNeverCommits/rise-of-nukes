☢ RISE OF NUKES

A tiny Rise of Nations style strategy game. Gather, grow, conquer, and nuke your friends.

How to play
  • Left-click to select, drag to box select. Right-click to move, attack, or send citizens to work.
  • Citizens build. Pick a building on the bottom bar, then click the map. Farms go near cities, camps go next to forests, mines go on grey metal rocks, oil wells go on black oil pools.
  • Your city trains citizens and moves you to the next age. New ages unlock better units and buildings.
  • Build more cities to grow your territory. You can only build inside your borders. Enemy territory slowly hurts your units.
  • Destroy an enemy city with soldiers nearby to capture it. Lose all your cities and you are out.
  • In the Atomic Age, research the Manhattan Project at a Library, build a Missile Silo, build a nuke, and launch it. Watch the Doomsday Clock: too many nukes and everyone loses.
  • Watch out for cows, supply crates, meteors, frogs and other surprises. Kill cows for food. Do not let cows walk into nuclear fallout.
  • Type a number (1-25) in chat to send a spoken taunt. Try 11, 14 and 22.
  • Keys: arrow keys / screen edge / middle-drag to scroll, mouse wheel to zoom, A attack-move, S stop, H home, . next idle citizen, Ctrl+1-9 make group, 1-9 select group, Enter chat, Space last alert.
