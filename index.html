☢ RISE OF NUKES

A tiny Rise of Nations style strategy game. Gather, grow, conquer, and nuke your friends.

How to play
  • Left-click to select, drag to box select. Right-click to move, attack, or send citizens to work.
  • Citizens build. Pick a building on the bottom bar, then click the map. Farms go near cities, camps go next to forests, mines go on grey metal rocks, oil wells go on black oil pools.
  • Your city trains citizens and moves you to the next age. New ages unlock better units and buildings. To advance you need a Library and its next Science tech.
  • Each resource has a gather limit shown at the top of the screen. Commerce techs at the Library raise it and unlock building upgrades. Markets raise it too, and let you swap resources.
  • Citizens can also build Wonders (J). There's only one of each in the game. Finish the Moon Rocket, keep it alive for 4 minutes, and your team wins.
  • Build more cities to grow your territory. You can only build inside your borders. Enemy territory slowly hurts your units.
  • Destroy an enemy city with soldiers nearby to capture it. Lose all your cities and you are out.
  • In the Atomic Age, research the Manhattan Project at a Library, build a Missile Silo, build a nuke, and launch it. Watch the Doomsday Clock: too many nukes and everyone loses.
  • Pick a nation in the lobby: Germany, Taiwan, Philippines or Pakistan. Each has a national power, unique units, a signature unit and a national tech.
  • Watch out for cows, supply crates, meteors, frogs and other surprises. Kill cows for food. Do not let cows walk into nuclear fallout.
  • Press Enter or 📣 for chat and spoken taunts. Start a message with /t to talk to your team only. Z or Alt+click pings the map for your allies.
  • Keys: arrow keys / screen edge / middle-drag to scroll, mouse wheel to zoom, A attack-move, S stop, H home, . next idle citizen, , idle soldiers, Ctrl+A whole army, Shift+right-click queue moves, Ctrl+1-9 make group, 1-9 select group, Enter chat, Space last alert, Pause or F8 pause.
